# AI Product Talking Points (CampusPe)

## 1. Lightning Fast Inference
"Our AI engine is powered by Groq's LPU™ (Language Processing Unit), delivering Llama 3.3 70B responses at speeds up to 10x faster than traditional cloud providers. No more waiting for 'thinking' bars—our reports generate in near real-time."

## 2. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
"We don't just guess. Our system uses a sophisticated RAG pipeline with ChromaDB to ground AI responses in your specific compliance documentation. Every answer is backed by your actual data, significantly reducing hallucinations."

## 3. High-Fidelity Reporting
"The `/generate-report` endpoint doesn't just return text; it builds a structured, actionable compliance roadmap. It categorizes requirements, suggests resources, and provides executive-level summaries automatically."

## 4. Scalable Architecture
"Built as a microservice using Flask and Redis, the AI service is designed for horizontal scale. Whether you're processing one report or a thousand in batch mode, CampusPe remains responsive."

## 5. Security & Privacy
"By utilizing Groq's secure API and maintaining local vector embeddings in ChromaDB, we ensure that your sensitive compliance data is handled with enterprise-grade privacy standards."
